Video, sound and messages

LiveKit carries live media and participant metadata. This application does not record audio or video to disk by default. Participants can see or hear what you share and may make their own copies.

Browser storage keeps language and device preferences and tab-scoped access credentials. Cookies support admitted file access and administrator sessions.

Ordinary chat stays in meeting browsers. Uploaded files are available to admitted members while their room access is valid. Configured file retention: ask the organizer.

Optional translation

Live translation sends microphone audio from the speaking browser to the configured speech provider. Translated audio travels through LiveKit. Optional chat translation sends the relevant text to the configured text provider; the original stays available. Provider processing and retention depend on the operator’s agreements.

Transcript and AI summary consent

Persistent capture starts off. When the installation and host enable it, each speaker may allow their own captions to be saved. A separate choice allows saved text to be sent for an AI summary. Joining a meeting gives neither permission.

You can withdraw consent in the meeting. Withdrawing transcript consent deletes your saved rows and invalidates summaries. Copies already sent to a provider, emailed or downloaded cannot be recalled. Transcripts may be incomplete or inaccurate; AI summaries require review.

Configured transcript retention: ask the organizer. The operator controls scheduled cleanup and backup retention.

Requests and schedules

If booking is enabled, the server stores the name, email, chosen time and other details you submit. Calendar and email services are used only when configured. Keep the private management link safe: it permits cancellation and rescheduling.

Pending requests expire after the configured waiting period. Booking details are retained for the configured period, measured from the later of creation or the booked time.

Usage measurements

The server retains request counts and provider-reported token totals. Browsers may report approximate audio and session durations. These measurements support resource limits and cost estimates; they do not contain spoken text and are not provider invoices. Configured usage retention: ask the organizer.

Questions, access and deletion

Ask the organization that invited you about the data controller, processors, regional policies, access or deletion requests and backups. An open source license does not make every installation compliant with privacy law.
